Monolith HMI Wiring

Every connection for the ESP32-S3 N16R8 board, the 2.8" ILI9341 touch display, the HW-040 encoder, the TTP223 touch key, the ZS-042 RTC and the AHT10, plus the 12 V supply. I chose the GPIOs to avoid the board's reserved pins and to keep wire crossings to a minimum.

Signal wiring

The pins are drawn in the board's physical header order, antenna end at the top and the USB-C ports at the bottom. Power pins on the modules carry a rail flag instead of a wire. The power section below shows how those rails are wired.

Pins left alone

  • 35 36 37 used internally by the N16R8's octal PSRAM. Using them crashes the board.
  • 0 3 45 46 strapping pins that set the boot mode. A module pulling one of them at reset can stop the board booting.
  • 19 20 the native USB-C port ("USB").
  • 43 44 UART0 to the CH343 chip on the "COM" USB-C port. Keep it for flashing and logs.

Build notes

  1. Set the buck first. Power the MP1584 alone from 12 V and turn its trimmer to 5.3 V with a multimeter. Only then connect anything to it.
  2. Two SS34 diodes. D1 in the 12 V+ wire protects the buck against a reversed plug, because the MP1584 has no protection of its own. D2 between buck OUT+ and the board's 5V pin stops USB power flowing back into the buck when you flash over USB-C. D2 does not stop the 5 V rail feeding the PC, so unless you know your board has a diode on VBUS, unplug 12 V while USB is connected.
  3. Display power. Feed TFT VCC from 5 V and leave the J1 solder jumper on the display open, so its own 3.3 V regulator runs. The display's logic is 3.3 V, so the data lines can go straight to the ESP32.
  4. Leave the display's SDO (MISO) open. On these modules the ILI9341 doesn't release MISO when not selected, which corrupts touch readings. Only T_DO goes to GPIO 13. You never need to read back from the display.
  5. Encoder on 3.3 V only. The HW-040's pull-up resistors go to its + pin, so 5 V there would put 5 V on GPIO 4–6. If the encoder skips steps, add a 100 nF capacitor from CLK to GND and another from DT to GND.
  6. ZS-042 with a CR2032. The module has a charging circuit (a 200 Ω resistor in series with a diode, near the battery) that tries to charge the coin cell. Remove that resistor when fitting a normal non-rechargeable CR2032.
  7. AHT10 on its own bus. The AHT10 is known to misbehave when it shares an I²C bus with other devices. A second bus costs two pins you have to spare. Its module already has pull-up resistors.
  8. Wire. Use 22 AWG for 12 V, 5 V and the buck-to-board GND, and 26–28 AWG silicone wire for signals. Keep the SPI wires to the display under about 15 cm. Put the display, encoder and touch key on plug-in connectors so the bezel can come off for service.

Firmware pin config

For Arduino with the TFT_eSPI library. The defines go in TFT_eSPI's User_Setup.h, the rest in your sketch.

// ---- TFT_eSPI  User_Setup.h ----
#define ILI9341_DRIVER
#define TFT_MOSI 11
#define TFT_SCLK 12
#define TFT_MISO 13            // wired to T_DO only
#define TFT_CS   15
#define TFT_DC   18
#define TFT_RST  17
#define TFT_BL    9
#define TFT_BACKLIGHT_ON HIGH
#define TOUCH_CS 10
#define SPI_FREQUENCY       40000000
#define SPI_READ_FREQUENCY  20000000
#define SPI_TOUCH_FREQUENCY  2500000
// if the S3 resets as soon as tft.init() runs, also add:  #define USE_HSPI_PORT

// ---- pins.h ----
constexpr int PIN_ENC_A     = 4;   // HW-040 CLK
constexpr int PIN_ENC_B     = 5;   // HW-040 DT
constexpr int PIN_ENC_SW    = 6;   // LOW while pressed
constexpr int PIN_BACK_KEY  = 16;  // TTP223, HIGH while touched
constexpr int PIN_TOUCH_IRQ = 14;  // XPT2046, LOW while pressed
constexpr int PIN_RTC_SDA   = 42, PIN_RTC_SCL = 2, PIN_RTC_SQW = 1;
constexpr int PIN_AHT_SDA   = 40, PIN_AHT_SCL = 41;

void setupPins() {
  pinMode(PIN_ENC_SW,    INPUT_PULLUP);
  pinMode(PIN_BACK_KEY,  INPUT);
  pinMode(PIN_TOUCH_IRQ, INPUT_PULLUP);
  pinMode(PIN_RTC_SQW,   INPUT_PULLUP);       // SQW is open-drain
  Wire.begin(PIN_RTC_SDA, PIN_RTC_SCL, 400000);   // DS3231 0x68, AT24C32 0x57
  Wire1.begin(PIN_AHT_SDA, PIN_AHT_SCL, 100000);  // AHT10 0x38
}
